AFFLUENCE FACTS
======================================================================
Affluence is an integer 1-4. It is probably a price level (1 = cheap ... 4 = expensive) but this is an assumption.
Only 1,026 places (9.5%) have it, and only places with 4+ reviews.

  level   places   share    avg rating   median reviews
  1         298    29.0%     3.96        74
  2         674    65.7%     3.98        411
  3          41     4.0%     4.14        1021
  4          13     1.3%     4.26        45

Level 2 dominates (66%); level 4 is rare (13 places).
Rating rises slightly with affluence (3.96 at level 1 -> 4.26 at level 4), but levels 3-4 have few places so be careful.
Spearman correlation affluence vs rating: 0.12;  affluence vs reviews: 0.52
33% of places with an affluence value are in Greater Dhaka (vs 12% of all places).
